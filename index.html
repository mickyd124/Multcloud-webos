MultCloud TV

Connect to your shared Orbit / MultCloud companion on your iPhone or computer to browse and play your own cloud videos. You can also open the official MultCloud website.

Open MultCloud Cloud setup help
Select MultCloud TV or Both in iSH. Enter the companion access token in the cloud library Settings screen. Both apps share cloud accounts. This is an unofficial companion client, not the official MultCloud API; the official website remains separate. Use private Wi-Fi only.
